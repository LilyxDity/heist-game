THE MIDNIGHT VAULT is a one-room stealth heist game in the browser: cross the vault hall of the Meridian Gallery past three patrolling guards and two sweeping security cameras, lift the Star of Meridian off its pedestal, and slip out through the service exit before the alarm brings the police. Read the vision cones before you move, sneak behind the pillars and display cases, spend smoke bombs and an EMP where they buy you the most time — every gadget comes off your final score.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Press Enter or click START THE HEIST: WASD or arrows to move, Shift to sneak, E to grab loot, Q for a smoke bomb, F for the EMP, P to pause, M to mute, R to retry. No build step, no network at play time — every file the game loads lives under `game/`.
